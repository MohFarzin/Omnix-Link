Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · https://relaylink.omnixstudio.xyz

1. Overview

Omnix Link (Relay Link) helps you authorize YouTube, Twitch, and Kick and hand tokens to your self-hosted Omnix StreamRelay. This policy explains what we collect on the Link website.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: email, password hash (if you register with email), display name, and optional Google / Discord identifiers from login.
  • Relay link: tunnel token metadata, link status, last-seen time, and the IP address shown when your egg dials in for confirmation.
  • Platform Connect: short-lived OAuth tokens while they are queued for encrypted handoff to your StreamRelay. We do not keep them as a permanent vault after delivery.
  • Technical logs: basic request logs (time, path, status) for security and reliability.

3. How we use data

  • Sign you in and protect your account
  • Confirm and maintain your one StreamRelay connection
  • Complete Connect flows and deliver tokens to your egg
  • Prevent abuse and debug outages

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not need your StreamRelay to expose a public IP or open inbound ports
  • Video content itself is processed on your StreamRelay, not stored by Omnix Link

5. Sharing

We share data with OAuth providers (Google, Twitch, Kick, Discord) only as needed to complete Connect / login you start. Hosting and CDN providers that run this site may process traffic metadata. We do not share account data with advertisers.

6. Retention

Account and relay records stay while your account exists. Queued platform tokens are removed after handoff or shortly after. You can Remove access for a platform on the Link site. Contact us to request account deletion.

7. Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. Token handoffs to your egg use an additional AES-256-GCM layer keyed by your connect token. No method is perfect; protect your Link password and connect token.

8. Children

Omnix Link is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Do not use the service if you are under that age.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: admin@omnixstudio.xyz

Also see our Terms of Service.
